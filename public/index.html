FAQ

Before you ask — answered

Which program should I start with?
Completely new to AI? Start with AI Training Foundations. Comfortable with prompting but new to code? Take AI Agent Building. Want to build websites end-to-end? Choose Full-Stack Website Building. Want everything plus career support? The AI Expert Track covers all three.
Are the classes live or recorded?
Both. Every program has a weekly live session with a mentor — and everything is recorded the same day, so you can catch up in your own timezone.
Do I get a certificate?
Yes — each program ends with a verifiable certificate of completion, earned by shipping the final project (not by attendance). The AI Expert Track adds a mentor-reviewed portfolio assessment.
What if I fall behind or miss a week?
Life happens. All sessions are recorded, projects have catch-up windows, and you can move to the next cohort once for free. Community members often team up to keep each other on pace.
What’s the refund policy?
Full refund within 14 days of any paid cohort — no questions asked, even if you already attended the live sessions.
Can I pay in installments?
Yes. Any paid tier can be split into 3 interest-free monthly installments — choose it at checkout, no extra paperwork.
